    Why a delay?

    The screenshot is taken when you press Ask, and Ask is on this page. A short delay gives you time to switch to the window with the problem first, and you'll hear a beep each second. Choose Immediately if you're sharing only that window.

    Let the assistant view one window so it can point to the exact control you need.

    Help & privacy

    • Share the window or tab that has the problem, not this page. If you share your whole screen, press Ask and then switch to the problem before the screenshot is taken.
    • A screenshot is attached only when you send a message while sharing. You can see it under the answer.
    • Screenshots go to Gemini to be read. Recordings go to ElevenLabs to be turned into text. This app doesn't save them.
    • Don't share windows that show passwords or private information.
